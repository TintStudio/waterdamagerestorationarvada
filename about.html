Rooted in Arvada Since 2014

Local Property Restoration Backed by Psychrometric Science & Integrity

Water damage is one of the most disruptive emergencies a Colorado property owner can face. Founded in 2014 in Arvada, Water Damage Restoration Arvada was established with a singular mission: to provide Jefferson and Adams County residents with an honest, scientifically grounded, and compassionate restoration partner.

Operating from our primary dispatch headquarters at 8105 Ralston Rd, Arvada, CO 80002, our mobile water extraction and structural drying units are positioned right in the heart of our community. Over the past 12+ years, our certified technicians have successfully mitigated more than 1,400 residential flooded basements, frozen pipe bursts, and commercial facilities across Olde Town, Leyden Rock, Whisper Creek, and Lake Arbor.

Unlike distant franchise conglomerates that treat property owners like numbers, our technicians live in the neighborhoods we serve. We treat every home, basement, and storefront with the exact same care, rigor, and respect we expect for our own families.

How We Operate

Our Core Restoration Principles

From first dispatch to final clearance testing, we hold ourselves to uncompromising standards of technical execution and customer service.

Speed Without Cutting Corners

Water migration causes progressive structural damage every hour it sits. We mobilize within minutes to stop active leaks, extract standing water, and deploy drying equipment before mold has the opportunity to colonize.

Average arrival under 60 minutes

Psychrometric Science

We never guess if a wall or subfloor is dry. We use calibrated pin and pinless moisture meters, FLIR thermal imaging, and daily psychrometric dry logs to verify structural moisture baselines before equipment is removed.

IICRC S500 & S520 Compliant

Advocacy for Property Owners

Navigating disaster recovery is stressful. We assist with complete insurance photographic documentation, Xactimate line-item estimates, and direct billing so you can focus on putting your life back together.
