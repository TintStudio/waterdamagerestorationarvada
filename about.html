Local Property Restoration Backed by Science & Integrity

Water damage is one of the most disruptive emergencies a homeowner or business owner can experience. Whether caused by a frozen pipe burst during a harsh Colorado freeze, a storm runoff basement flood, an unexpected water heater failure, or a commercial kitchen fire, the emotional and financial strain is immediate.

Water Damage Restoration Arvada was established with a singular mission: to provide the Arvada and northwest Denver Metro communities with an honest, scientifically grounded, and compassionate restoration partner. Headquartered at 8105 Ralston Rd, Arvada, CO 80002, we are positioned right in the heart of our community, enabling our mobile extraction units to arrive on site quickly when every minute matters.

Unlike franchise conglomerates that treat property owners like transaction numbers, our certified technicians live in the neighborhoods we serve. We treat every home, basement, office, and storefront with the exact same care, rigor, and respect we would expect for our own families.

How We Operate

Our Core Restoration Principles

From first dispatch to final clearance testing, we hold ourselves to uncompromising standards of technical execution and customer service.

Speed Without Cutting Corners

Water migration causes progressive structural damage every hour it sits. We mobilize within minutes to stop active leaks, extract standing water, and deploy drying equipment before mold has the opportunity to colonize.

Average arrival under 60 minutes

Psychrometric Science

We never guess if a wall or subfloor is dry. We use calibrated pin and pinless moisture meters, FLIR thermal imaging, and daily psychrometric dry logs to verify structural moisture baselines before equipment is removed.

IICRC S500 & S520 Compliant

Transparent Insurance Advocacy

We work directly with all major homeowners and commercial property insurance carriers. We generate comprehensive Xactimate line-item estimates and photo logs so your claim is processed smoothly without surprise fees.

Industry Credentials

Certified IICRC Master Restorer Standards

Restoration is an exact science that demands rigorous technical knowledge of building materials, airflow dynamics, thermodynamics, and microbial ecology. Our team holds active certifications from the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC) under Master Restorer #48291.

ANSI/IICRC S500 Standard for Water Damage

Governs structural drying, water categorization (Category 1, 2, and 3 black water), and humidity control.

ANSI/IICRC S520 Standard for Mold Remediation

Standard of care for containment barriers, HEPA filtration, antimicrobial sanitization, and clearance verification.

Fire & Smoke Damage Restoration (FSRT)

Specialized soot removal chemistry, thermal fogging, hydroxyl deodorization, and structural stabilization.

Why Certification Matters

Protecting your property value and health

Improper drying leaves hidden moisture inside wall cavities and subfloors, resulting in dry rot, warped framing, and toxic black mold growth weeks after the initial leak. Certified restoration guarantees that drying is verified through standardized scientific measurements, protecting your home's structural integrity and your family's health.

State-of-the-Art Restoration Equipment

We invest in premier industrial restoration technology to expedite the drying curve and eliminate hidden moisture pockets.

FLIR Thermal Cameras

High-resolution infrared thermal imaging reveals cold moisture signatures trapped behind drywall and under hardwood without tearing walls open.

LGR Dehumidifiers

Low-Grain Refrigerant and desiccant units draw dozens of gallons of airborne moisture out daily even in deep humidity baselines.

High-Power Extractors

Truck-mounted vacuum systems and submersible trash pumps extract high-volume flood water from basements and crawlspaces rapidly.

HEPA 500 Air Scrubbers

Multi-stage HEPA filtration captures 99.97% of airborne spores, dust, and particulate down to 0.3 microns during remediation.
