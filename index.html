Urgent Property Threat

Water Damage Can Get Worse Quickly

Water flows along the path of least resistance, quickly penetrating behind baseboards, seeping into subfloors, and saturating insulation. What starts as a minor plumbing leak or appliance overflow can rapidly evolve into warped hardwood, ceiling sagging, structural framing damage, and hazardous mold growth.

Within 1-2 Hours: Water spreads into drywall, swelling wood framing and saturating carpets.
Within 24-48 Hours: Drywall begins breaking down, metal surfaces tarnish, and mold spores begin germinating.
Within 48+ Hours: Severe structural degradation and Category 3 bio-contamination risks increase.
Professional Extraction: Commercial LGR dehumidifiers and Injectidry systems prevent tear-out costs.
