Proven Restoration Protocol

How Our Water Damage Restoration Process Works in Arvada

Six steps from first assessment to written proof your property is dry.

1 Step 01

Step One: Assess

We evaluate your Arvada property to locate the source of the water and determine how far it has spread. No extraction work begins until the source is identified and, if needed, stopped.

2 Step 02

Step Two: Inspect

Moisture meters and thermal imaging scan walls, flooring, and hidden cavities so you get an accurate reading of how deep the water has traveled. This confirms exactly what we're dealing with before any equipment is brought in.

3 Step 03

Step Three: Extract

Truck-mounted and portable extraction units remove standing water quickly, minimizing how much time moisture has to soak into flooring, drywall, and furniture.

4 Step 04

Step Four: Dry

Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers are placed strategically to dry every affected surface — not just the areas that look wet, but the hidden moisture behind walls and under floors.

5 Step 05

Step Five: Monitor

We track moisture levels daily using professional-grade meters, adjusting equipment placement as needed until every reading confirms the space is fully dry.

Final Clearance

Step Six: Confirm

A final moisture check verifies the property is completely dry and free of hidden water damage. You receive a written clearance report as your permanent proof the job was done right.

Frequently Asked Questions About Water Damage Restoration

Get clear answers to common questions regarding water mitigation, repair costs, insurance coverage, and emergency restoration procedures.

Water damage remediation vs. restoration: what's the difference?
Remediation focuses on removing water and drying the space to stop further damage, while water damage restoration goes further — repairing or rebuilding damaged structures like drywall, flooring, and ceilings to return the property to its original condition. Learn more about our structural drying services.
How do I know if my water damage is serious?
Signs of serious water damage include warped flooring, sagging ceilings, persistent musty odors, visible mold, and discoloration on walls. If water sat for more than 24–48 hours, call our emergency team immediately at (720) 735-8908 for a professional assessment or explore our emergency restoration services.
Can water damage make you sick?
Yes, standing water and resulting mold growth can cause respiratory issues, allergic reactions, and other health problems, especially with prolonged exposure or contaminated (black) water. Explore our certified mold remediation and mold removal solutions.
What's the difference between inspection, assessment, and mitigation?
Inspection is a visual check for water intrusion and moisture. Assessment is a detailed evaluation of damage extent and category. Mitigation is the active process of extracting water and drying the property to prevent further loss through certified water damage restoration.
Do you serve Arvada and nearby suburbs?
Yes, we provide 24/7 water damage restoration throughout Arvada and surrounding communities including Westminster, Wheat Ridge, Golden, Lakewood, Broomfield, and the greater Denver metro.
What does IICRC certification mean?
IICRC certification means technicians are trained to industry-standard protocols for water damage restoration and structural drying — ensuring safe, proper handling of Category 1, 2, and 3 water damage.
Is water damage restoration covered by homeowners insurance?
Most policies cover sudden, accidental water damage like a burst pipe, but usually exclude damage from long-term leaks or lack of maintenance. Coverage depends on the cause and your specific policy. Call our insurance claim specialists directly at (720) 735-8908 for direct billing assistance.
Can I stay home during water damage restoration?
In most cases, yes, especially for contained, single-room damage. For larger jobs involving major structural drying or contaminated water, temporary relocation may be recommended.
How fast can you respond to a water damage emergency?
We offer 24/7 emergency response and can typically be on-site within 60–90 minutes for active water damage situations in Arvada. Call our immediate dispatch line now at (720) 735-8908.
What areas of a home get hit by water damage most?
Basements, bathrooms, kitchens, and areas around plumbing, water heaters, and roofing are most prone to water damage due to pipe leaks, appliance failures, and storm or flood intrusion.
How much does water damage restoration cost?
Water damage restoration typically costs $1,300–$6,000 depending on the affected area's size, water category, and how long the water sat before treatment. Larger structural damage can exceed $10,000.
