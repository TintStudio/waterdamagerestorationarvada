Urgent Property Threat

Water Damage Can Get Worse Quickly

Water flows along the path of least resistance, quickly penetrating behind baseboards, seeping into subfloors, and saturating insulation. What starts as a minor plumbing leak or appliance overflow can rapidly evolve into warped hardwood, ceiling sagging, structural framing damage, and hazardous mold growth.

Within 1-2 Hours: Water spreads into drywall, swelling wood framing and saturating carpets.
Within 24-48 Hours: Drywall begins breaking down, metal surfaces tarnish, and mold spores begin germinating.
Within 48+ Hours: Severe structural degradation and Category 3 bio-contamination risks increase.
Professional Extraction: Commercial LGR dehumidifiers and Injectidry systems prevent tear-out costs.

Systematic Approach

Our 5-Step Water Mitigation & Drying Process

We follow industry-standard structural drying guidelines to return your property to safe moisture baselines.

01

Inspection & Mapping

Thermal cameras and pinless moisture meters detect hidden moisture pockets behind walls and under floors.

02

Water Extraction

High-powered truck-mounted vacuums extract thousands of gallons of standing water quickly to minimize absorption.

03

Structural Drying

Commercial LGR dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers draw moisture out of drywall, framing, and hardwood.

04

Cleaning & Sanitizing

EPA-registered botanical antimicrobials sanitize all affected surfaces to prevent bacterial contamination and odor.

05

Final Clearance

Daily psychrometric logs verify structural dry standards before drying equipment is demobilized.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about water damage extraction and drying.

First, locate and shut off the main water valve to stop the flow. Next, turn off electricity to the flooded area if it is safe to do so. Move furniture and valuable items to dry ground, and call our emergency dispatch line at (720) 735-8908 so our technicians can extract standing water before secondary structural damage begins.
On average, complete structural drying takes between 3 to 5 days, depending on the volume of water, affected materials (hardwood, drywall, concrete), and ambient temperature. Moisture levels are measured and documented daily using calibrated moisture meters to ensure full drying before equipment is removed.
Most standard homeowner's policies cover sudden and accidental water damage, such as burst plumbing pipes, frozen pipe breaks, or appliance supply failures. Gradual leaks or external floodwaters typically require specific flood riders. We document all moisture data and loss reports to assist your adjuster during claims processing.
Mold spores can begin germinating within 24 to 48 hours in moist organic materials like drywall, carpet backing, and wood framing. Immediate water extraction and rapid deployment of commercial dehumidifiers are critical to keep relative humidity below spore germination thresholds.
