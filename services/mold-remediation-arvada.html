Safe Containment & Indoor Air Protection

Mold spores become airborne immediately when disturbed, easily spreading to unaffected areas of your property through HVAC ductwork. Never attempt to scrape or spray bleach on large mold infestations without containment.

We build zippered polyethylene containment barriers under continuous negative air pressure, capture microscopic airborne spores with 99.97% efficient HEPA scrubbers, and safely remove contaminated porous materials before applying EPA-registered mold inhibitors.
