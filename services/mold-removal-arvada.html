Physical Material Removal & Framing Encapsulation

When mold hyphae deeply penetrate porous building substrates like drywall and fiberglass insulation, surface cleaning alone is insufficient. The contaminated material must be double-bagged and removed under sealed negative pressure.

After physical removal, exposed structural stud framing is abrasive-sanded, HEPA-vacuumed, and sealed with antimicrobial encapsulants to prevent recurrent fungal colonization.
